# March

## March 1 — The Day of the First Breath

*Pisces I — The Week of Spirit · Water · Mutable · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You start things from feeling rather than from a plan, and it works more often than it has any right to. Something moves in you and you follow it. The trouble comes when the feeling fades halfway through and there's no plan underneath to carry you the rest of the way.

**Love**  You know quickly, and that instinct is usually sound about the person even when it's wrong about the timing. Check it against a few months of actual behavior before you rearrange your life around it.

**Money**  You earn on inspiration rather than routine, which means good months and empty ones in no predictable order. Set up something automatic so a flat stretch doesn't sink you while you wait for the next surge.

**Family**  You're the one who starts the traditions — the thing that happens every year now was your idea once and nobody else would have thought of it. That's a real contribution even if it doesn't look like work.

**Emotions**  Feeling is your engine, and without it you genuinely can't move at all. That's a real vulnerability, because a flat stretch doesn't just slow you down, it stops you.

**Friendships**  You draw people in fast and easily. Keeping them takes the follow-through you find hardest, which is why your circle turns over more than you'd like.

**Mind**  You need something that moves you. Meaningless work damages you more than hard work ever could, and you'll take a pay cut for meaning without hesitating.

**Body**  Energy comes with enthusiasm and goes when it goes. Build a routine that survives a low week, because the ones built during your good weeks always assume you'll stay there.

**Spirit**  You act on what you sense, and that's already a practice whatever you call it. Most people override their instincts and you've never learned how.

**Work**  Best where you can start things and worst where you have to maintain them. Bring in somebody who finishes, and pay them properly, because they're doing the half you can't.

**Risk**  You leap on feeling — good instincts, thin planning. Add one page of actual plan before the next one and your hit rate goes up without costing you the instinct.

**Outlook**  Something you begin on impulse becomes the main thing. It won't be the one you'd currently bet on, and that's been the pattern every time.

**Strengths**  inspired · quick to begin · genuine

**Weaknesses**  runs on feeling · no plan · loses steam

**Advice**  Write down the plan while the feeling is still hot. You'll need it when it cools.

> The spark isn't the fire.

## March 2 — The Day of the Still Pool

*Pisces I — The Week of Spirit · Water · Mutable · 2 Moon · The High Priestess*

You know things without being told. Not magic — you've been watching people closely since you were small, and you've got a memory for what they do rather than what they say. It makes you quietly one of the most perceptive people in any room you're in.

**Love**  You see your partner clearly, including what they hide from themselves. Be careful what you do with that, because being known that thoroughly is unnerving as well as valuable.

**Money**  Cautious and quiet about it, which has served you well and kept you out of things that took other people down. Just check the caution hasn't become avoidance.

**Family**  You knew the family secrets before anybody told you, and you've probably never mentioned that. Carrying knowledge nobody knows you have is its own weight.

**Emotions**  You take in everyone else's and then can't locate where yours end and theirs begin. Learning to separate them is the single most useful skill available to you.

**Friendships**  You're the one people confide in and very few return the favor, and you've stopped expecting them to. That imbalance is worth naming with at least one person.

**Mind**  Quiet is not optional for you — noise and crowds drain you in a way they don't drain others. Protect it and stop treating the need for it as antisocial.

**Body**  Rest and stillness matter more for you than exertion, and sleep especially. Everything you're picking up all day has to be processed somewhere.

**Spirit**  You already have a strong inner sense and trusting it is the only remaining step. You've spent years checking it against evidence and it's been right.

**Work**  Anywhere reading people is the job — negotiation, care, management, teaching. You'd be wasted on pure numbers and slightly miserable.

**Risk**  You hold back and watch. Occasionally you need to move on what you've seen rather than just noting it and being right later.

**Outlook**  It comes to you slowly and it stays. Late recognition that doesn't go away, which is the better kind.

**Strengths**  perceptive · calm · trustworthy

**Weaknesses**  holds back · absorbs others · invisible

**Advice**  Act on what you've seen. Noticing isn't the same as doing something about it.

> Still water reflects best.

## March 3 — The Day of the Locked Room

*Pisces II — The Week of the Loner · Water · Mutable · 3 Jupiter · The Empress*

You're warm and generous and you still need to be alone more than the people around you understand. Those two things aren't in conflict — you give a lot when you're out, and you need real solitude to refill. People take the door closing personally and it was never about them.

**Love**  You need a partner who doesn't take your alone time as rejection. Say it out loud early, in plain words, because they will absolutely interpret it wrong otherwise.

**Money**  You spend on comfort and on making your space right, and that's not indulgence for you — it's maintenance. A home that works is a functioning requirement rather than a luxury.

**Family**  You love them from your own room, and they may read the distance as something it isn't. One sentence explaining it would save years of quiet misunderstanding.

**Emotions**  Warm and deep, processed privately. Nothing gets sorted in a crowd, so waiting until you're alone isn't avoidance — it's the only method that works for you.

**Friendships**  A few, warm, seen rarely, and they pick right back up where you left off. That's the good kind and you've cultivated it without calling it a strategy.

**Mind**  Alone time is your medicine. Guard it and stop apologizing, because the apologizing is what signals to people that it's up for negotiation.

**Body**  Comfort suits you and so does actual movement, which you'll skip in favor of comfort if allowed. Make the movement comfortable and you'll keep it.

**Spirit**  Your inner life is rich and it doesn't need an audience or a group. Whatever happens in that room is the practice and it's been going on for decades.

**Work**  Independent work, own hours, own space. Open offices genuinely ruin you and every job where you've struggled probably had one.

**Risk**  You play it safe to protect your peace, which is understandable. One well-picked chance would repay you and wouldn't cost the peace you're guarding.

**Outlook**  Comfortable and quiet, and that's not settling for you — it was always the goal. Other people's ambitions were never yours.

**Strengths**  warm · self-sufficient · generous

**Weaknesses**  retreats · hard to reach · misread as cold

**Advice**  Tell people the door isn't about them. They've been guessing for years.

> A full cup was filled somewhere quiet.

## March 4 — The Day of the Built Wall

*Pisces II — The Week of the Loner · Water · Mutable · 4 Uranus · The Emperor*

You built defenses young and they worked, which is why they're still up. You're soft underneath and structured on the outside — rules, routines, a way things are done. That structure is genuinely protecting something, and the question at this point is whether it's still needed at the same height.

**Love**  It takes a long time to get in, and it's worth it for whoever manages. Hard on the ones who gave up early, and some of them would have been good for you.

**Money**  Careful, organized, private, and you'll be fine. Nobody who knows you has any idea what your position actually is.

**Family**  You keep them at a set distance, and that distance was set a long time ago by something specific. Worth remembering what, because the reason may not apply anymore.

**Emotions**  Enormous feeling behind a firm door, and both are real. The door isn't a lie about what's inside, it's just a door.

**Friendships**  Small and old. New people rarely get past the first layer and you've mostly stopped noticing that you're not letting them.

**Mind**  The wall costs energy to maintain, continuously, whether or not anything's attacking. Notice how much of your daily reserve goes to holding something up.

**Body**  You hold everything in your body. Regular release matters more for you than intensity, and the tension is chronic rather than situational.

**Spirit**  You'll work it out yourself on your own terms, and nothing gets accepted on somebody else's word. That's slower and it's yours.

**Work**  You want autonomy and structure at once, which sounds contradictory and isn't. Your own operation gives you both and every employer has given you one or neither.

**Risk**  You avoid it to protect what you built. The risk actually worth taking is letting one new person in, which costs less than you're pricing it at.

**Outlook**  Softer later. The wall comes down a few courses at a time and each one is a relief rather than an exposure.

**Strengths**  self-protecting · organized · deep

**Weaknesses**  walled off · rigid · slow to trust

**Advice**  Test one section of the wall. See if the thing it was built against is even still out there.

> A wall built at ten is still ten years old.

## March 5 — The Day of the Inside Voice

*Pisces II — The Week of the Loner · Water · Mutable · 5 Mercury · The Hierophant*

There's a running commentary in your head that never stops, and it's sharper and funnier than what you actually say out loud. Most people get the edited version. The unedited one is the interesting one, and letting a bit of it out is what turns acquaintances into friends.

**Love**  You're funnier and stranger than you let on, and a partner who's only met the edited version is with somebody who doesn't exist. Let them meet the other one.

**Money**  You think about it far more than you act on it — plans, calculations, scenarios that never leave your head. Fewer plans and more moves would change your position.

**Family**  You've held your tongue at family gatherings for decades, and some of that was right. Some of it was you deciding in advance that they couldn't handle it.

**Emotions**  You analyze instead of feeling, and those are genuinely different activities. The analysis is so good that you can go years mistaking it for having processed something.

**Friendships**  The ones you've let the real version out to are the ones that stuck, every time. That's the whole mechanism and you've never quite drawn the conclusion.

**Mind**  Your mind won't quiet down on command. Writing works better for you than any attempt to stop it, because it gives the commentary somewhere to go.

**Body**  Nervous energy in a sensitive system. Regular gentle movement rather than hard bursts, and daily rather than occasional.

**Spirit**  You want something with actual structure to it. Loose spirituality does nothing for you and you'd rather have a system with edges you can push against.

**Work**  Writing, analysis, anything where the private thinking is the actual product. You've been doing it for free your whole life.

**Risk**  You rehearse instead of acting, sometimes for years. At some point say it before you've polished it, because the polished version arrives too late.

**Outlook**  What you've been working out privately turns out to be worth something to other people. You'd never have guessed that and it's been true the whole time.

**Strengths**  sharp mind · observant · original

**Weaknesses**  over-thinks · self-censors · stays in your head

**Advice**  Say one unedited thing today. That's where the real friendships come from.

> The best line stayed in your head again.

## March 6 — The Day of the Long Attachment

*Pisces II — The Week of the Loner · Water · Mutable · 6 Venus · The Lovers*

You attach to few people and permanently. It's not that you're closed off — it's that once someone's in, they're in for life, and that kind of loyalty makes you very careful about who you let start. You'd rather have three people forever than thirty for a while.

**Love**  You love for a long time, including well after it ends. Give yourself explicit permission to stop, because you won't take it otherwise.

**Money**  You spend on the few and you're indifferent to everyone outside the circle. That's not meanness, it's the same concentration you apply to everything.

**Family**  Whether family is blood or chosen, it's the center of your life either way. Everything else arranges itself around that and always has.

**Emotions**  Deep, slow-moving, long-lasting. Nothing about you is a phase, which is why the losses take years rather than months.

**Friendships**  Three or four people, decades each, and that's the whole picture and it's enough. You've never once wanted a wider circle and you've been told you should.

**Mind**  Your steadiness depends on those few relationships. Trouble in one of them shows up everywhere else in your life within a week.

**Body**  You do better with company — a walking partner, someone to go with. Alone, you skip it, reliably, every time.

**Spirit**  Love as practice, plain and direct, no doctrine required. That's an old tradition even if you'd never file it under that heading.

**Work**  Loyal past the point of sense, and check occasionally whether the job still deserves it. You'll stay somewhere for the people long after the work stopped being right.

**Risk**  You won't risk the people, which is understandable. Just don't call that a reason to never risk anything, because it's a different question.

**Outlook**  The few stay. You do not end up alone, and that was never actually in question.

**Strengths**  deeply loyal · warm · constant

**Weaknesses**  holds on too long · narrow circle · stubborn

**Advice**  Loving someone doesn't oblige you to stay in the arrangement. Those are separable.

> Few doors, and none of them ever close.

## March 7 — The Day of the Far Room

You're somewhere else a good part of the time, and the somewhere else is more detailed than anybody would guess. You're not drifting — you're working on something in there. What's tricky is bringing it out into the world, because it never looks the same once it's out.

**Love**  You need someone who doesn't mind you disappearing mid-conversation and understands it isn't about them. Explaining that once is worth more than apologizing for it fifty times.

**Money**  You'll fund the private project first and worry about the bills after. Reverse that occasionally, because the project doesn't survive the household failing.

**Family**  The one with their head in the clouds, according to them, and they're half right. The other half is a level of work they've never seen.

**Emotions**  You retreat inward to process, and it works, and it's slow. People waiting for a response from you are often waiting on something that's genuinely underway.

**Friendships**  Few, patient, and they've learned to wait you out. That patience is the entry requirement and it filters hard.

**Mind**  The inner world can carry you or swallow you — same place, different weather. Learn the signs of which one you're in, because from inside they feel similar.

**Body**  You forget you have a body when you're deep in something. A basic daily habit, set as a fixed rule rather than a decision, is the only fix.

**Spirit**  This is where you actually live and everything else is arrangements. Most people would call that escapism and they've never been in there.

**Work**  Creative, independent, unsupervised. You'd be miserable anywhere else and you've probably tested that more than once.

**Risk**  You take them privately and nobody knows until after. Tell one person before the next one, not for permission but for a witness.

**Outlook**  Something you've been building alone in there gets made real. That's the whole arc of your life and it takes longer than you'd like.

**Strengths**  imaginative · driven inside · original

**Weaknesses**  absent · hard to reach · struggles to finish

**Advice**  Get one thing out of your head and into the world this month, badly if necessary.

> A room nobody's seen might as well be empty.

Your life shifts in ways you don't control and mostly can't predict. You've learned to move with it rather than brace against it, which is a genuine skill. What still catches you is how alone you are in each turn — you handle the change and you handle it by yourself.

**Money**  In and out. Save in the good stretch, because you know from direct experience what follows and you've still been caught out.

**Family**  They've watched you change several times and stopped trying to keep up. That's not indifference, it's them giving up on a moving target.

**Emotions**  You move with it — highs are real and lows genuinely pass. That's worth knowing when you're at the bottom, which is exactly when you won't believe it.

**Friendships**  Different people in each chapter, and keeping one from an old one would give you a reference point you currently lack.

**Mind**  Bottom of a turn is where you're hardest on yourself and least accurate. Make no assessments about your life down there.

**Body**  Cycles rather than a flat line. Work with them instead of fighting them, which you've been doing for years without success.

**Spirit**  You've seen it turn enough times to trust that it does. That's evidence-based rather than hopeful, which makes it sturdier than most beliefs.

**Work**  Several different things and each one led somewhere, though only visibly in hindsight. Stop apologizing for the shape of the record.

**Risk**  You'll go again after a loss, which is fine as long as something actually changed in between. Repeating the same bet isn't resilience.

**Outlook**  It turns up. It has every time and there's no evidence that this is the exception.

**Strengths**  adapts · resilient · self-reliant

**Weaknesses**  goes it alone · low in the trough · restless

**Advice**  Let somebody be there for the next turn. You don't have to ride it out solo.

## March 31 — The Day of the Set Jaw

*Aries I — The Week of the Child · Fire · Cardinal · 4 Uranus · The Emperor*

You decide and that's the end of the discussion. There's real power in that — you don't waver, you don't relitigate, and people can build plans around your word. It becomes a problem the day you're wrong, because you'll go a long way down the road before you'll turn around.

**Love**  Once you've committed you don't waver, and that's a genuine gift for the right person. For the wrong one it's a decade neither of you wanted.

**Money**  You stick to a plan, which works. Just review the plan yearly, because sticking to a bad one is the same discipline pointed wrong.

**Family**  The one whose mind doesn't change. They've stopped arguing and that isn't the same as agreeing.

**Emotions**  Firm on the surface. What's underneath rarely gets shown and it's considerably more than the surface suggests.

**Friendships**  Loyal to the end. Cross a line and it's over permanently, and there's no appeals process.

**Mind**  Being told what to do is what actually gets to you. Pressure you handle fine — it's the instruction you can't take.

**Body**  You'll follow your own program forever and nobody else's for a week. Design it yourself and it'll hold for a decade.

**Spirit**  Accepted on your own authority or not at all. Nothing arrives complete and survives with you.

**Work**  You end up in charge. Build in someone who's explicitly allowed to tell you you're wrong, because otherwise nobody will.

**Risk**  You commit hard and don't hedge, which has paid off and also cost you. Both, in roughly equal measure.

**Outlook**  You build something solid with your stamp on it and it lasts. That's the point of you.

**Strengths**  decisive · steady · reliable

**Weaknesses**  won't reverse · rigid · resists being told

**Advice**  Pick one person who's allowed to tell you you're wrong. Then actually listen to them.

> The crown fits. Take it off sometimes anyway.
