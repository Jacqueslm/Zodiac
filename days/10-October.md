# October

## October 1 — The Day of the Set Bar

*Libra I — The Week of the Perfectionist · Air · Cardinal · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You set the standard for whatever room you're in and you do it by example rather than instruction. People raise their game around you without being asked. That's real influence and you've probably never counted it as a skill.

**Love**  You hold a high bar. Make sure your partner agreed to it rather than simply inheriting it when they met you.

**Money**  Your quality is worth more than you charge. You've been pricing the hours rather than the standard.

**Family**  The one everyone measures against, which is a compliment and a weight on the people doing the measuring.

**Emotions**  You want them to be reasonable and they aren't. That isn't a flaw in the emotions.

**Friendships**  People try harder around you. Some find that energizing and some find it exhausting, and you rarely know which.

**Mind**  You need something worth doing properly. Low-stakes work leaves you flat and irritable.

**Body**  A specific routine, held. Once it exists it lasts for years without effort.

**Spirit**  Doing it right as a practice. That's a complete answer and you've never needed another.

**Work**  You set the tone for whatever room you're in. Get paid for that as well as for your output.

**Risk**  You back your own judgment and you're mostly correct. That's earned rather than assumed.

**Outlook**  What you set stays set. People carry your standard forward into places you'll never see.

**Strengths**  exemplary · self-starting · high standards

**Weaknesses**  exacting · impatient · hard to be near

**Advice**  Say out loud that the bar is yours, not universal. It relieves everyone.

> You raised the room without saying a word.
