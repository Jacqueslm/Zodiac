## May 1 — The Day of the Solid Start

*Taurus I — The Week of Manifestation · Earth · Fixed · 1 Sun · The Magician*

When you start something it's already half-built in your head — materials, cost, how long. You don't announce until you can deliver. That's why your track record is better than people who talk more, and it's also why nobody knows how much you've got going on.

**Love**  You show up rather than say things, and the showing up is real. A partner may need the words as well, and giving them costs you nothing you can't spare.

**Money**  You start with a real plan and you fund it properly, which is why you rarely fail outright. The discipline is the whole edge.

**Family**  The one who actually gets things done at family level — the house, the arrangements, the money. Everyone else discusses and you handle it.

**Emotions**  Slow to move and hard to shift once moved. That's stability in the good stretches and it's why the bad ones last so long.

**Friendships**  Few, practical, decades long. You've never advertised for new ones and never needed to.

**Mind**  You need a project with an end product you can point at. Abstract work leaves you flat regardless of how important it's supposed to be.

**Body**  Strong and steady, and once a routine takes hold it holds for years. Getting it started is the only difficult part.

**Spirit**  Making something real out of an idea is close enough to it for you. That's a complete answer rather than a substitute for one.

**Work**  Founder or builder rather than talker. You'll build the thing while other people are still pitching it, and you'll finish first.

**Risk**  You calculate then commit fully — good style. Just don't calculate for three years, because the calculating can become the project.

**Outlook**  What you start exists in ten years. That's rarer than it sounds and it's your actual distinguishing feature.

**Strengths**  practical · self-starting · thorough

**Weaknesses**  slow to launch · quiet about it · stubborn

**Advice**  Start before you're ready once. See that the world doesn't end.

> Everything you need is already on the bench.
