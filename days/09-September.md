# September

## September 1 — The Day of the Working Plan

*Virgo I — The Week of System Builders · Earth · Mutable · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You start with a plan that actually works — costed, sequenced, allowing for what goes wrong. Other people start with enthusiasm. That's why your projects survive contact with reality and theirs don't.

**Love**  You approach it practically, which is unromantic and effective. Warmth matters too and you're capable of it when you remember to show it.

**Money**  Planned and funded properly, which is why you rarely fail outright. The discipline is the whole advantage.

**Family**  The one who organizes everything and thinks of what nobody thought of. That's invisible until you're not there.

**Emotions**  You handle them like a problem to be solved. Some of them aren't problems and won't respond to the method.

**Friendships**  Few, practical, long. You help rather than talk and the help is worth more.

**Mind**  You need a project with a plan. Vagueness leaves you flat and irritable in a way you can't explain to anyone.

**Body**  A specific routine held for years once it's started. The starting is the only difficult part.

**Spirit**  Making a plan real is close enough to it for you, and that's a complete answer rather than a dodge.

**Work**  Planning and building. You're the one who makes it actually happen after everyone else has finished describing it.

**Risk**  You calculate first, which is right. Don't calculate for three years, because the calculating can quietly become the project.

**Outlook**  What you start still exists in ten years. That's the thing that actually distinguishes you and it's never impressed anybody at a party.

**Strengths**  organized · practical · thorough

**Weaknesses**  over-plans · slow to launch · treats feelings as tasks

**Advice**  Start before the plan's finished once. It won't ruin anything.

> The plan is not the thing.
