# August

## August 1 — The Day of the Taken Lead

*Leo I — The Week of Authority · Fire · Fixed · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You step into the gap. When nobody's leading, you are — not because you wanted the job but because the silence was unbearable. That's why you've ended up in charge of things you never applied for.

**Love**  You lead in the relationship by default rather than by agreement. Check your partner is fine with that, because they may have adapted rather than chosen.

**Money**  You back yourself and it's mostly worked. The times it hasn't, you'd already committed before anyone could question it.

**Family**  In charge whether or not you're the oldest. That got settled by temperament and nobody has ever revisited it.

**Emotions**  Strong, fast, out in the open. Everyone knows where you stand and nobody has to guess.

**Friendships**  You organize everyone. It's a job as much as a role and nobody has ever offered to take a turn.

**Mind**  You need something to run. Idle is genuinely bad for you and most of your worst decisions were made in empty stretches.

**Body**  Strong energy that needs regular use. Without an outlet it turns into restlessness and then into temper.

**Spirit**  You believe you can make it happen, which has been mostly true. Worth remembering it's a belief rather than a law.

**Work**  In charge whether or not it's the title. Better to have the title, because responsibility without authority is the worst combination available.

**Risk**  You back yourself heavily. Keep one thing permanently off the table, because your style has no natural floor under it.

**Outlook**  You end up running something and it suits you. The question is whether you chose it or just filled a vacancy.

**Strengths**  natural leader · decisive · confident

**Weaknesses**  takes over · impatient · won't share control

**Advice**  Let someone else lead one thing. Watch what they do differently.

> You stepped in because nobody else did. That's still a choice.

You've got the ability to lead and a real preference for not being out front. That combination makes you the best second-in-command anyone could have, and it also means you've watched people less capable than you get the top job.

**Friendships**  Deep and few. People confide in you and rarely notice you haven't confided back.

**Mind**  You need quiet. Noise wears you down faster than any amount of responsibility does.

**Spirit**  Strong inner life that you'd never call that. It's been running quietly for decades without a name.

**Strengths**  capable · loyal · perceptive

**Money**  Straight dealing. It's cost you a little and bought you a reputation that money couldn't have.

**Friendships**  People trust you to tell them the truth, which means they come to you when they suspect they don't want to hear it.
