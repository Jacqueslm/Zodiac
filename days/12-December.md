# December

## December 1 — The Day of the Clear Route

*Sagittarius I — The Week of Independence · Fire · Mutable · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You see where you're going before anyone else has agreed there's a direction. That clarity is why people follow you, and it's why you're often out ahead alone, wondering where everybody went.

**Love**  You're direct about what you want, which is refreshing and occasionally arrives faster than the other person can process.

**Money**  You back your own read and it's mostly paid. Keep a reserve, because your confidence doesn't come with a cushion attached.

**Family**  The one who says where things are headed. Sometimes they needed to arrive at that themselves.

**Emotions**  Fast, hot, over quickly. Everyone else is still catching up when you've already moved on.

**Friendships**  You gather people easily and maintain them poorly. That's the whole pattern and it's fixable.

**Mind**  You need a direction. Without one you're not restless, you're genuinely lost, and it shows quickly.

**Body**  Strong energy needing regular use. Without an outlet it turns into impatience with people.

**Spirit**  You believe the way through exists and you'll find it. That's carried you through things that stopped others.

**Work**  Setting direction and getting things moving. Bring in someone who handles the middle stretch.

**Risk**  You go early and confidently. Add one page of plan and your hit rate rises without costing the nerve.

**Outlook**  You end up somewhere worth being. The route will look nothing like the one you described at the start.

**Strengths**  clear-sighted · decisive · leads

**Weaknesses**  outpaces people · impatient · doesn't finish

**Advice**  Check who's actually behind you before you go around the next corner.

> Seeing the road isn't the same as walking it.
