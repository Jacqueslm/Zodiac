# July

## July 1 — The Day of the Protective Start

*Cancer I — The Week of the Empath · Water · Cardinal · 1 Sun · The Magician*

You start things to protect people. A business so the family's secure, a project so somebody's looked after, a move so the kids get something better. Almost nothing you've built was purely for you, and that's worth noticing at some point.

**Love**  You provide and protect, and that's real love expressed the only way you know. Your partner may want you present more than provided for, and those aren't the same thing.

**Money**  Everything you earn has somebody's name on it besides yours. That's admirable and it means you've never once asked what you'd want if the answer only involved you.

**Family**  The one who built the safety net. They've been standing on it for years without looking down at it.

**Emotions**  Strong and channeled into doing rather than saying. The doing is genuine and it isn't always legible to people who need words.

**Friendships**  You look after people and few look after you. That arrangement settled early and nobody's revisited it.

**Mind**  You need to be protecting something. Without that you feel useless rather than free, which is worth knowing before the kids leave.

**Body**  You'll run yourself down for others, for years, and only stop when something makes you. Get on the list before it does.

**Spirit**  Providing is your practice and it's legitimate. It also isn't everything, and there's a version of you that exists outside it.

**Work**  You start things and keep them running because people depend on them. That dependency is both the fuel and the trap.

**Risk**  You take them for others and not for you. Take one that's just yours and notice how unfamiliar that feels.

**Outlook**  What you built protects people for a long time, longer than you'll be around to see it working.

**Strengths**  protective · self-starting · devoted

**Weaknesses**  never for himself · over-responsible · silent

**Advice**  Start one thing that's only for you. See how strange that feels.

> You can't shelter anyone from under the same roof.

**Money**  Cautious. You avoid what others walk into, and that avoidance has never once looked like an achievement to anybody.

**Family**  You knew everything nobody said, from very young. That's a heavy thing for a child to carry and you carried it without comment.

**Emotions**  Yours and everyone else's, tangled together. Some of what you're feeling isn't yours and separating them is the one skill worth building.

**Friendships**  Everyone confides in you and rarely the reverse. Worth naming that with one person rather than accepting it permanently.

**Mind**  Quiet is required. Crowds and noise drain you fast and it isn't a preference you can override with willpower.

**Work**  Anywhere reading people is the job. You'd be underused anywhere technical and slightly unhappy about it.

**Risk**  You hold back and adjust. Sometimes set the temperature instead of reading it — you'd be surprised how easily a room follows.

**Outlook**  Slow gains that don't go away. Late recognition of the permanent kind.

**Strengths**  deeply perceptive · calm · safe to be around

**Weaknesses**  absorbs everything · reactive · invisible
