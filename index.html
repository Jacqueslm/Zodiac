How this works, and what's original here

The 48 periods and their date ranges come from this project's README and are used exactly as written. The correspondences are the standard personology framework: each period's sign gives its element and quality; the day of the month reduces to a number 1–9 with its ruling planet; and the day of the month also names a Major Arcana card (days 1–22 directly, days 23–31 by digit sum). Every word of interpretation — the readings, the paths, the pair profiles — was written for this app. Nothing is quoted from any book, and the path names here are this app's own, not any published list.
